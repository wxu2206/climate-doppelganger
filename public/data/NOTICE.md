# Data attribution

Climate observations: NASA Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER),
NASA Langley Research Center, https://power.larc.nasa.gov/.
The project is independent and not endorsed by NASA. POWER combines NASA Earth
science inputs; preserve response header citations and consult POWER's current
methodology for parameter-specific products and grid resolutions.

Map land geometry: Natural Earth 1:110m land, public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Downloaded from the Natural Earth project's official vector repository:
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
This is cartographic geometry, not a NASA environmental measurement.

The included climate.json is an authentic NASA POWER/MERRA-2 snapshot for 10 requested points, 1991–2024. Original request URLs, source metadata, retrieval timestamps and raw SHA-256 hashes are embedded. A small genuine response used by tests retains its provenance notice in tests/fixtures/nasa-power-vancouver-2024.provenance.json.
